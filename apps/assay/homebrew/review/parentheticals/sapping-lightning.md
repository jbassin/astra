# Sapping Lightning

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(basic Reflex save)`

**Rank 7** · antillurgy, concentrate, electricity, manipulate

**Traditions:** arcane, occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Area:** 120-foot line  
**Defense:** basic reflex  

---

You unleash a beam of antillurgic energy from your fingertips in a sizzling 120-foot line.
Each creature in the line takes 9d12 electricity damage (basic Reflex save).
A failing creature also has its highest-rank currently prepared, spontaneous, or innate spell slot suppressed rather than consumed, meaning it cannot cast that spell for the suppression's duration.
Creatures without spellcasting are unaffected by the suppression.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage and its spellcasting is not suppressed.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and its highest-rank spell slot is suppressed until the end of its next turn.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and its two highest-rank spell slots are suppressed until the end of its next turn.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The electricity damage increases by 1d12.
