# Mass Fluency

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(5 at 7th, 6 at 8th, etc.)`

**Rank 6** · concentrate, linguistic, manipulate, memetics, mental

**Traditions:** arcane, occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** up to 4 willing creatures touched one at a time  
**Duration:** 1 week  

---

You inscribe a memetic language-matrix into the minds of up to four willing creatures, touching each in turn as part of the casting.
For the duration, each target can understand any language they read or hear, interpreting it perfectly regardless of dialect or cipher.
In addition, each target can speak in any language they understand through this spell as long as they are physically capable of speech.
The effect is purely linguistic.
It does not grant cultural context, decode intentional obfuscation, or reveal meaning Hidden beneath the literal words.

---
**Heightened (+1)** You can affect one additional willing creature (5 at 7th, 6 at 8th, etc.).
