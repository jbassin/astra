# Reset

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(this is a fortune effect)`

**Rank 4** · chronomancy, concentrate, fortune, manipulate, misfortune

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Targets:** up to 4 creatures  
**Defense:** will  

---

A burst of temporal energy ripples outward from you, seizing the threads of fate around each target and forcing a re-evaluation of their place in the combat order.
For each target, the effect depends on whether they are your ally or your enemy:

**Ally:** The target may immediately reroll their initiative, taking the higher of the new roll or their current initiative value (this is a fortune effect).
This takes effect at the start of the next round.

**Enemy:** The target must attempt a Will save against this misfortune effect.
New initiative results take effect at the start of the next round.

A creature can only be affected by Reset once per encounter.

**Critical Success** The enemy is unaffected and temporarily immune to Reset for 24 hours.

**Success** The enemy is unaffected.

**Failure** You choose which of the two rolled initiative values applies to the enemy.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the enemy also becomes Off-Guard to all creatures until the end of its next turn as temporal disorientation washes over it.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The number of targets increases by 1.
