## Features

### Species (Orc)
- **Adrenaline Rush** ☐ ☐ — Bonus action: Dash and gain 2 temp HP. 2 uses, regained on short/long rest.
- **Relentless Endurance** ☐ — Reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright: drop to 1 HP instead. 1/long rest.
- **Darkvision** — 180 ft (orc 120 + Shadow Arts 60).

### Monk
- **Martial Arts** — Unarmed strikes and monk weapons (simple melee + light martial melee) use d6 + Dex.
- **Dexterous Attacks** — Bonus-action unarmed strike; Dex sets grapple/shove save DC (14).
- **Flurry of Blows** — 1 focus point: two unarmed strikes as a bonus action.
- **Patient Defense** — Bonus action Disengage; or 1 focus point: Disengage + Dodge.
- **Step of the Wind** — Bonus action Dash; or 1 focus point: Dash + Disengage, jump distance ×2.
- **Uncanny Metabolism** ☐ — When rolling initiative: regain all focus points and heal 4 + 1d6. 1/long rest.
- **Deflect Attacks** — Reaction when an attack hits with B/P/S damage: reduce that damage by 1d10 + 8.
  - **Redirect:** if reduced to 0, spend 1 focus point — a foe within 5 ft (melee) / 60 ft (ranged) makes a Dex save DC 13 or takes 2d6+4 of the attack's type.
- **Slow Fall** — Reaction: reduce fall damage by 20.

### Warrior of Shadow
- **Shadow Arts: Darkness** — 1 focus point: cast *Darkness* (no components), you can see inside it, move it 60 ft at the start of each of your turns.
- **Shadowy Figments** — You know *Minor Illusion* (Wis).

### Feats
- **Tavern Brawler** — Reroll 1s on unarmed strike damage dice; push 5 ft once per turn on an unarmed hit (Attack action); proficient with improvised weapons.
