# Suspension

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(floor, wall, ceiling, cliff face, hull of a ship, etc.)`
> - `(such as a shove, bull rush, or gust of wind)`
> - `(using a reaction or if you lose consciousness)`

**Rank 4** · concentrate, gestalt, manipulate, plant

**Traditions:** primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** self  
**Targets:** you  
**Duration:** 5 minutes  

---

Vines magically sprout from any solid surface within 30 feet of you and weave around your torso, leaving your arms and legs free.
The vines extend and retract as you move, always anchoring to the nearest surface.
For the duration, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain a fly Speed of 40 feet, provided you remain within 30 feet of a solid surface (floor, wall, ceiling, cliff face, hull of a ship, etc.). If you move farther than 30 feet from any solid surface, this fly Speed ends immediately and you fall normally.
- You cannot be knocked Prone.
- You gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against effects that would force you to move against your will (such as a shove, bull rush, or gust of wind).
- If you are falling when you cast this spell (using a reaction or if you lose consciousness), the vines catch you, negating all falling damage.

---
**Heightened (5th)** The required proximity to a surface extends to 60 feet, and the fly Speed increases to 60 feet.
