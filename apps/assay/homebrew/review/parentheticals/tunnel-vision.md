# Tunnel Vision

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(the fortune trait applies; additional fortune effects cannot further alter the roll)`

**Rank 1** · chronomancy, concentrate, fortune, manipulate, mental

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 willing creature  
**Duration:** 8 hours  

---

You imbue a willing creature with an acute foresight that sharpens its reflexes just before danger strikes.
For the duration, the target gains a +1 status bonus to initiative rolls.
Additionally, once during the duration, the target can invoke the foresight as a free action at the start of a combat to roll initiative twice and take the higher result (the fortune trait applies; additional fortune effects cannot further alter the roll).
Once the fortune effect is used, only the +1 status bonus remains.
If you Cast this Spell again, any prior casting of it ends.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The status bonus to initiative increases to +2, and the fortune re-roll can be used twice instead of once.
