# Fast-Forward

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(attack rolls, skill checks, saving throws)`
> - `(permanent until the curse is removed)`

**Rank 6** · chronomancy, concentrate, curse, incapacitation, manipulate

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** fortitude  
**Duration:** until removed  

---

You reach into a creature's personal timeline and hurl it decades into the future, leaving its body wizened, aged, and Enfeebled while its mind remains trapped in the present.
The target must attempt a Fortitude saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature's apparent age advances rapidly and then snaps back over 1 round.
It is Slowed 1 until the start of your next turn.

**Failure** The creature is cursed with accelerated aging.
It takes a –2 status penalty to Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution-based rolls (attack rolls, skill checks, saving throws) and its Speed is reduced by 10 feet.
This curse persists until removed.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the penalty is –3 to all affected rolls and the Speed reduction is 20 feet.
Additionally, the creature is Enfeebled 2 for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (8th)** On a failure, the creature is also Drained 2 (permanent until the curse is removed). On a critical failure, it is Drained 3 instead.
