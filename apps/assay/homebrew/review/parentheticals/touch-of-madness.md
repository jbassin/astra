# Touch of Madness

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(following the last spoken order given to it unless compliance would cause self-harm)`
> - `(no curse)`
> - `(as the confusion condition for 1 round)`
> - `(permanent until counteracted)` `[duration.value]`

**Rank 8** · concentrate, curse, incapacitation, linguistic, manipulate, memetics, mental

**Traditions:** arcane, occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** will  
**Duration:** varies (permanent until counteracted)  

---

You overwhelm a creature's mind with a cascade of contradictory impulses and shattered cognition.
The target must attempt a Will save.
On a failure, the target is afflicted with the Fractures of Madness curse: it becomes Stupefied 4, cannot Cast Spells, and treats all non-hostile creatures as friendly (following the last spoken order given to it unless compliance would cause self-harm).
The curse persists indefinitely and can be removed only through effects that target curses, such as Cleanse Affliction; this requires a successful counteract check against this spell's rank.
The target may attempt a new Will save to end the curse at each monthly interval.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is Stupefied 2 for 1 minute (no curse).

**Failure** The target is afflicted with the Fractures of Madness curse, as described above.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the target immediately Strides toward the nearest visible creature and attacks it, regardless of allegiance (as the confusion condition for 1 round), before the longer-term curse takes hold.
