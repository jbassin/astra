### Marginal rider price (GM Core's -1-rank rule)

Adding a SIGNIFICANT condition rider to an otherwise-priced damage spell discounts the total to roughly **×0.50–×0.75** of what the same-rank pure budget would otherwise buy — GM Core's own 'apply a condition ≈ damage of 2+ creature levels lower' rule, reproduced empirically by round 1's pure-vs-rider probe (rider-family spells deal ×0.43–0.81 of same-rank pure budget) and consistent with round-2's Stage A T2/T3 β fits landing well under 1.0. A flat guidance rate, not per-condition — apply it to the SPELL'S total budget, not to any one atom.
