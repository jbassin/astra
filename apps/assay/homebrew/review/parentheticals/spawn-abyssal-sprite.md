# Spawn Abyssal Sprite

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(double on a critical hit)`

**Rank 7** · attack, concentrate, manipulate, planara, void

**Traditions:** arcane, divine, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature, then chaining to additional creatures  

---

You crack a demon's finger bone and hurl an abyssal bolt of void energy at a creature you can see within range.
Make a spell attack roll against the target.
On a hit, the bolt deals 12d6 void damage (double on a critical hit).
The bolt then leaps to another creature of your choice within 30 feet of the previous target that has not already been struck by this casting; make a new spell attack roll for each subsequent target.
The chain continues until you miss or no eligible target remains within 30 feet of the last target struck.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial damage increases by 2d6.

**Heightened (+1)** damage +2d6
