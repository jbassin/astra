# Divine Regression

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(healing spells, fast healing, regeneration, potions, the Treat Wounds activity targeting it)`
> - `(treating partial minutes as full)`
> - `(counteract rank 2)`

**Rank 2** · antillurgy, attack, concentrate, manipulate, void

**Traditions:** divine, occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** 1 minute  

---

You reverse the flow of vital energy within a creature, weaponizing its own capacity for recovery.
Make a melee spell attack roll against the target.
On a hit, the target takes 3d8 void damage and is afflicted with Reversed Vitality for 1 minute.
While afflicted, any time the target would regain HP from any source (healing spells, fast healing, regeneration, potions, the Treat Wounds activity targeting it), it instead takes void damage equal to the amount it would have healed, and regains no HP.
At the end of each of the target's turns, it can attempt a Fortitude save against your spell DC; a success reduces the duration remaining by half (treating partial minutes as full), and a critical success ends the effect immediately.
The affliction also ends if effects that target curses, such as Cleanse Affliction, are used to counteract it (counteract rank 2).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial void damage increases by 1d8.
