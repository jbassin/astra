# Shape Modify: Armor

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(or another polymorph effect)`
> - `(you cannot Dash or use Sudden Charge)`
> - `(total: Speed reduced by 5 feet)`

**Rank 3** · concentrate, gestalt, manipulate, morph

**Traditions:** primal  
**Cast:** 1 action  
**Range:** self  
**Targets:** you  
**Duration:** until your wild shape ends  

---

You draw upon the collective's memory of wildshaping to refine your current form.
You must be in wild shape (or another polymorph effect) to Cast this Spell; if you are not, the spell fails with no effect.
You reinforce the exterior of your current form into thick, rigid plates, trading agility for protection.
For the duration, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC in your current form, but your Speed is reduced to 15 feet for all movement types (you cannot Dash or use Sudden Charge).
This spell ends immediately when you leave your polymorphed form.

---
**Heightened (5th)** The circumstance bonus to AC increases to +3, and your Speed penalty is lessened: your Speed is reduced by 10 feet rather than being set to 15 feet.

**Heightened (7th)** The circumstance bonus to AC increases to +4.
The Speed penalty is reduced by a further 5 feet (total: Speed reduced by 5 feet).
