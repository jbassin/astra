# Cerebral Disruption

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(d6, rolled by the GM or chosen thematically)`
> - `(permanent until counteracted)` `[duration.value]`

**Rank 9** · concentrate, curse, emotion, fear, incapacitation, manipulate, memetics, mental

**Traditions:** arcane, occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** will  
**Duration:** varies (permanent until counteracted)  

---

You reach into the target's mind and rewire its cognitive architecture, leaving it permanently diminished.
The target must attempt a Will save.

The curse persists indefinitely.
It can be removed only through effects that target curses, such as Cleanse Affliction; this requires a successful counteract check against this spell's rank.
The permanent Stupefied condition can be removed only by magic that restores the mind, such as Sound Body, which likewise requires a successful counteract check against this spell's rank.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target becomes Stupefied 2 for 1 minute.

**Failure** The target becomes Stupefied 4 permanently and is afflicted with one Cerebral Disruption curse effect (d6, rolled by the GM or chosen thematically): 1: the target is Frightened 2 whenever it can perceive you; 2: the target develops a severe phobia and is Frightened 2 in the presence of a common object the GM names; 3: the target automatically critically fails Intelligence-based skill checks; 4: the target is Confused for 1 minute, then must save against your spell DC each day or be Confused for 1 round; 5: the target suffers amnesia and cannot Recall Knowledge or use class abilities that require memory of its training; 6: the target undergoes childlike mental regression, and the GM controls its non-combat social behavior.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the target is Confused for 1 round.
