# Checkpoint

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(including death effects, Massacre's death-on-crit-fail, or similar)`
> - `(as a rank-9 2-action Heal)`
> - `(the spell ends)`

**Rank 9** · chronomancy, concentrate, healing, manipulate, vitality

**Traditions:** arcane, divine, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 willing living creature  
**Duration:** 1 hour  
**Rarity:** rare  

---

**Requirements** The target is at full Hit Points.

You imprint a temporal checkpoint on the target's life-force, recording its current vitality.
For the duration, the target carries this temporal anchor.

If the target would be reduced to 0 HP or would die outright (including death effects, Massacre's death-on-crit-fail, or similar) at any point during the duration, instead of Dying or falling Unconscious, the checkpoint activates: the target immediately regains HP equal to 9d8+72 (as a rank-9 2-action Heal), any Dying and Wounded conditions are removed, and the temporal anchor is consumed (the spell ends).
The target's position does not change when the checkpoint activates.

The checkpoint activates only once per casting and then the spell ends.
If the duration expires without the checkpoint triggering, the spell ends with no effect.

---
**Heightened (10th)** The checkpoint restores the target to full HP instead of a fixed amount, and the duration increases to 8 hours.
