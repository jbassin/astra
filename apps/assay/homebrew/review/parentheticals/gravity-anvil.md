# Gravity Anvil

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(it can't use actions, reactions, or free actions)`
> - `(and the success damage by half that, and crit-fail damage doubles accordingly)`

**Rank 7** · concentrate, force, incapacitation, kosmoturgy, manipulate

**Traditions:** arcane, divine  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** fortitude  
**Duration:** varies  

---

You conjure an anvil of pure compressed gravity and slam it down on a single target you can see within range.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes 5d10 force damage.

**Failure** The creature takes 10d10 force damage and is Stunned 2.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 20d10 force damage and is incapacitated until the end of its next turn (it can't use actions, reactions, or free actions).
At the end of its turn, it may attempt another Fortitude save; on a success the incapacitation ends.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage on a failure increases by 1d10 (and the success damage by half that, and crit-fail damage doubles accordingly).
