# Awkward

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(and –3 on a critical failure)`

**Rank 4** · concentrate, emotion, manipulate, mental, mercuromancy

**Traditions:** divine, occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** will  
**Duration:** 1 minute  

---

You cause a creature to temporarily lose its social and physical confidence, making it obliviously Clumsy.
The targeted creature must attempt a Will save.
A creature affected by this spell doesn't notice it is performing poorly and can't use reactions to reroll or improve the affected checks.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature is affected by awkwardness.
It takes a –1 status penalty to attack rolls, Charisma-based skill checks, and Charisma-based saving throws.
It also can't benefit from fortune effects on those rolls.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the penalty increases to –2 and also applies to Dexterity-based saving throws.

---
**Heightened (6th)** On a failure, the penalty increases to –2 (and –3 on a critical failure).
