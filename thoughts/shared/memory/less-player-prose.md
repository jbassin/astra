Keep the text a player or reader sees short. When there is too much, delete it, reuse a line that
already exists, or let the app make the choice for them. Do not write replacement prose without
asking.

**Why:** on 2026-11-12 Josh asked to cut the character builder's text: "i dont want the
prospective players to do too much reading ... im generally unhappy with the prose that ai writes
but dont have the time to go over all of it so i just want to limit whats there". The builder
then showed up to 3,400 words per player. The cut (#231, #232) halved that with no new writing,
and he had the builder pick the species sub-choice rather than explain it.

**How to apply:** before adding summaries, hints or explanations to a player-facing page, count
what a reader already sees and ask whether a number, a name or nothing would do. Say each thing
once. Prefer text generated from data (a roll line) to written prose. When new prose is needed,
keep it to one line and list it for Josh to check. See [[no-silent-scope-cuts]].
