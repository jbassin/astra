# Arcane Censure

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(those with the spell trait or equivalent)`
> - `(focus points used for non-spell abilities, class features that don't involve spell slots)`
> - `(counteract DC = your spell DC)`
> - `(its magic is disrupted but not severed)`
> - `(no daily saves to recover)`

**Rank 9** · antillurgy, concentrate, curse, incapacitation, manipulate, mental

**Traditions:** arcane, occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature with spellcasting ability  
**Defense:** will  
**Duration:** varies (see text)  

---

You sever a creature's connection to the magical wellspring of its power, imposing the Arcane Censure curse.
The target must attempt a Will save.

The curse, if applied, prevents the target from Casting Spells and from using innate magical abilities (those with the spell trait or equivalent).
Other supernatural abilities (focus points used for non-spell abilities, class features that don't involve spell slots) are unaffected.
The curse can be ended by a rank-9 counteract attempt (counteract DC = your spell DC), or by a rank-9 restoration-class spell such as the 9th-rank Restore Senses or an equivalent.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is Stupefied 2 for 1 hour (its magic is disrupted but not severed).

**Failure** The target is afflicted with the Arcane Censure curse for 1 week, during which it cannot Cast Spells or use innate magical abilities.
At the end of each day, it may attempt a new Will save; on a success, the curse ends.

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with the Arcane Censure curse indefinitely (no daily saves to recover).
Only a counteract attempt at rank 9 or a specific restoration effect can end it.
