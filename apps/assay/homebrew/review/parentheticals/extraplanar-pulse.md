# Extraplanar Pulse

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(minimum 0)`

**Rank 2** · attack, concentrate, manipulate, planara, vitality

**Traditions:** arcane, divine, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** 1 minute  

---

You hurl a ball of pulsing violet light harvested from the space between planes.
Make a ranged spell attack roll against the target.
On a hit, the target takes 4d6 vitality damage.
Additionally, on a hit the target must attempt a Fortitude save against your spell DC; on a failure, the planar energy saturates the target, causing it to become Sickened 1 for 1 minute.
On a critical failure of the Fortitude save, the target is Sickened 2.
The target can spend an action to attempt a Fortitude save to reduce the Sickened condition by 1 (minimum 0).
On a critical hit with the attack roll, the Fortitude save is made at a -2 status penalty.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The vitality damage increases by 2d6.

**Heightened (+1)** damage +2d6
