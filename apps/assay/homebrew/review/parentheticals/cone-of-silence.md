# Cone of Silence

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(they simply hear nothing)`
> - `(such as casting spells with the auditory or linguistic trait)`
> - `(the sudden re-introduction of sound overwhelms it)`

**Rank 5** · auditory, concentrate, manipulate, memetics

**Traditions:** arcane, occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** self  
**Area:** 60-foot cone  
**Defense:** fortitude  
**Duration:** sustained up to 1 minute  

---

You stop all vibration from propagating through a cone-shaped volume of air that extends from your eyes; no sound can enter, exit, or propagate through the affected area.

Every creature in the area when you Cast the Spell, and every creature that enters the area, must attempt a Fortitude save when first affected.
A creature fully within the cone cannot use auditory actions, cannot make sound, and cannot receive auditory information from within the cone.
The cone follows your line of sight as you turn; creatures that leave the cone are no longer silenced.
The cone extends in the direction you are facing.

Sustaining the spell reorients the cone to face any direction you choose as part of the Sustain action.

Creatures within the cone are immune to thunder damage and automatically critically succeed at Perception checks that rely on hearing sounds originating within the cone (they simply hear nothing).

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected by the silencing.

**Success** The creature is Deafened while inside the cone.

**Failure** The creature is Deafened while inside the cone and cannot use auditory actions (such as casting spells with the auditory or linguistic trait) while inside the cone.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature is also Deafened for 1 minute after it leaves the cone (the sudden re-introduction of sound overwhelms it).

---
**Heightened (7th)** The cone extends to 120 feet. The silence is total even for creatures who succeed at the Fortitude save; a successful save grants immunity to the Deafened condition only, not to the silence itself.
