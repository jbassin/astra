# Containment Orbs

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(arcane, divine, occult, or primal)`
> - `(concentrate, manipulate)`
> - `(loaded or empty)`
> - `(4 orbs at 8th, 5 at 9th, etc.)`

**Rank 7** · antillurgy, concentrate, force, manipulate

**Traditions:** arcane, occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** self  
**Defense:** basic reflex  
**Duration:** 10 minutes  

---

Three small orbs of multicolored force orbit you.
When you cast Containment Orbs, assign each orb one magical tradition (arcane, divine, occult, or primal).
Each orb can absorb the next spell of its assigned tradition that targets you or includes you in its area, negating that spell entirely.
An orb can absorb only one spell and is then loaded.
As a 2-action activity (concentrate, manipulate), you can cause a loaded orb to detonate: all creatures within 20 feet of you take force damage equal to 3d6 per rank of the absorbed spell, with a basic Reflex save against your spell DC.
Each detonated orb is destroyed.
When the spell ends, any remaining orbs (loaded or empty) dissolve harmlessly.

---
**Heightened (+1)** You gain one additional orb (4 orbs at 8th, 5 at 9th, etc.).
