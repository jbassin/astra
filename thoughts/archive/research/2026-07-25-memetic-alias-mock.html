codex · homebrew · design decision R1

The Memetic Veil — four treatments

Each obfuscated name shows its alias under a dreamlike effect; hovering (or focusing) reveals the true name centered on the alias's footprint — the line never reflows, and a longer name extends past both ends of the alias over a parchment wash. Clicking pins the reveal. The spell card's real pair is “land of fae” ⇢ Feywild; each variant also carries a length test with a deliberately longer sample name, “the wood” ⇢ Neverwinter Wood.

Cast two actions · Range 120 feet · Area 10-foot burst

Defense Reflex · Duration varies (see below)

You summon a bubble from the that floats rapidly toward a point within range and detonates. All creatures within 10 feet of the point of impact must attempt a Reflex save. Creatures hit are covered with viscous fey goop.

Success The creature is unaffected.

Failure The creature is covered in bubble goop until it scrapes it off as described above.

Critical Failure As failure, and the penalty worsens to Clumsy 2.

Interaction contract (all variants)

  • Hover / focus — full reveal of the true name, centered on the alias's midpoint. The line never reflows; a longer name extends symmetrically past both ends of the alias, floating over a parchment wash so it stays legible.
  • Click, Enter, or Space — pins the reveal (gold underline); click again to re-veil. Pins reset on page load.
  • Reduced motion — all animation stops; the veiled word keeps a static tell so it still reads as obfuscated.
  • Foundry — displays the alias only; the module data never contains the true name.
  • Search — indexes the alias only. Searching “Feywild” finds nothing, by design.

“the wood” ⇢ “Neverwinter Wood” is an illustrative sample pair for the length test only, not store content. Fonts are stand-ins for the codex's EB Garamond / Cormorant SC / Oswald; the palette is the codex's own parchment token set.
