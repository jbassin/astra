# Propagating Blast

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(apply all weapon properties, traits, and any rune effects; this does not consume an additional action)`

**Rank 2** · concentrate, force, kosmoturgy, manipulate

**Traditions:** arcane, divine, occult  
**Cast:** 1 action  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Area:** 30-foot line  
**Defense:** basic reflex  
**Requirements:** You are wielding or holding a melee weapon  

---

You channel violent force through your melee weapon and launch a pulsing bolt that tears a line through space.
The bolt travels in a 5-foot-wide, 30-foot-long line originating from your space, passing through all creatures in the area.

Each creature in the line must attempt a basic Reflex save.
When the weapon-effect trigger applies, the affected creature suffers the effects of a successful melee Strike from the weapon used to cast this spell (apply all weapon properties, traits, and any rune effects; this does not consume an additional action).
Only one such weapon-effect trigger occurs per casting, applied to the first eligible creature in the line starting from you.

Damage: 2d8 force.

**Critical Success** The creature takes no damage.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage.
If it is the first failing creature in the line, it also suffers the effects of a successful melee Strike from the casting weapon.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage.
If it is the first failing creature in the line, it also suffers the effects of a successful melee Strike from the casting weapon.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The force damage increases by 1d8.

**Heightened (4th)** The weapon-effect trigger can apply to the first two failing creatures in the line rather than only one.

**Heightened (6th)** The weapon-effect trigger applies to the first three failing creatures in the line.
