# Eldritch Horror

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(you choose targets)`
> - `(Escape DC = your spell DC)`
> - `(maximum Drained 4)`

**Rank 9** · attack, concentrate, extradimensional, incapacitation, manipulate, planara, void

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 3 actions  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Area:** 10-foot burst  
**Defense:** reflex  
**Duration:** sustained up to 1 minute  

---

You tear open a portal to the Far Realms on the floor or ground at a point you can see within range.
The portal is a 10-foot-radius circle.
Creatures standing in the portal's space when it appears must attempt a Reflex save or be pulled through immediately; on a success they leap to the nearest safe space as the portal irises open.

Four alien tendrils emerge from the portal.
Each round when you Cast the Spell and at the start of each of your turns while you Sustain it, each tendril attempts to grab one creature within 30 feet of the portal (you choose targets); make a spell attack roll for each tendril.
A hit creature is Grabbed (Escape DC = your spell DC) and is dragged 10 feet toward the portal.
A tendril that already has a creature Grabbed drags it an additional 10 feet toward the portal instead of making a new attack.

Creatures inside the portal are in the Far Realms, removed from the plane where the portal was cast; they can't affect or be affected by anything outside the portal.
At the start of each of their turns inside the portal, they must attempt a Fortitude save against your spell DC.

**Critical Success** The creature inside the portal takes no damage this round.

**Success** The creature takes 4d6 void damage.

**Failure** The creature takes 8d6 void damage and becomes Drained 1, or increases its Drained value by 1 if it is already Drained (maximum Drained 4).

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature is Stunned 1.

A creature inside the portal can spend a single action to attempt an Athletics check or an Escape attempt against your spell DC; on a success, it climbs free and emerges in the nearest unoccupied space beside the portal.
When the spell ends, all creatures still inside reappear in the nearest unoccupied spaces to where the portal stood, alive or dead.
