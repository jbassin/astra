The Faerrin campaign (the live Foundry world) uses a modified Automatic Bonus Progression. The
modification: only potency, striking and resilient runes are removed. Items keep their item
bonuses (GM Core's ABP would strip them; Faerrin doesn't), and property runes are in play.

**Why:** a loot suggestion for the ESOTERICA fight offered +2 weapon potency runes; Josh corrected
it, then clarified that item bonuses stay (2026-11-16).

**How to apply:** when suggesting treasure, never offer potency, striking or resilient runes. Items
with item bonuses, property runes, spells, consumables and currency are all fine.
