# Oddly Satisfying

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(same DC)`
> - `(rather than the start)`
> - `(no longer requires touch)`

**Rank 5** · concentrate, emotion, incapacitation, manipulate, memetics, mental

**Traditions:** occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** will  
**Duration:** 1 minute  

---

You flood the target's mind with overwhelming feelings of contentment and satisfaction, a soothing psychic tide that makes action feel unnecessary.
The target must attempt a Will saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is Slowed 1 until the end of its next turn, as the wave of contentment briefly washes over it.

**Failure** The creature is overwhelmed by satisfaction.
For the duration, it is Slowed 1, takes a –2 status penalty to attack rolls and skill checks, and must succeed at a DC 5 flat check to use any action that would harm or threaten the caster or the caster's allies.
At the start of each of its turns, the creature can attempt a new Will saving throw (same DC); on a success, the effect ends.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is Slowed 2 and the flat check DC to act aggressively increases to 10.
The creature makes its per-turn Will save at the end of its turn (rather than the start) and must succeed twice to break free.

---
**Heightened (7th)** The range becomes 30 feet (no longer requires touch), and you can target up to 2 creatures.
