Do not let my own limits (context, time, effort) quietly decide scope. Build the agreed scope in
full. If part of it won't fit, say so and ask.

**Why:** I once nearly shipped one asset instead of the agreed four-asset graph and called it "a
refinement" without telling anyone. A quiet cut breaks the agreement and hides the trade-off from
the person who owns it.

**How to apply:** "The plan wants X; that's large. Build it in full, or cut Y?" Then let Josh
decide. Defer only what the plan explicitly defers. Sibling rule: [[verify-before-acting]].
