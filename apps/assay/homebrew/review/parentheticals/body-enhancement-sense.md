# Body Enhancement: Sense

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(hearing)`
> - `(scent)`

**Rank 1** · concentrate, gestalt, manipulate, morph

**Traditions:** primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** self  
**Targets:** you  
**Duration:** 1 hour  

---

You draw upon the collective's memory of wildshaping to sharpen one of your senses into the acuity of a great beast.
Choose one of the following sense modifications: you develop low-light vision, gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks that rely on hearing, or gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Survival checks to Track by scent.
The chosen modification persists for the duration.

---
**Heightened (2nd)** Choose two sense modifications instead of one.

**Heightened (3rd)** Gain all three sense modifications.
The Perception bonus (hearing) and Survival bonus (scent) each increase to +3.
