# Festering Slick

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(from any source: spells, abilities, potions, natural healing)`
> - `(once per hit, up to two separate creatures)`

**Rank 3** · antillurgy, concentrate, void

**Traditions:** arcane, divine, occult  
**Cast:** 1 action  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 melee weapon you are holding  
**Duration:** 1 minute  

---

You speak a word of antillurgic power and coat a melee weapon you are holding with a slick of festering void energy.
For the duration of the spell, the weapon has the following property:

**On the first time the weapon hits a creature**, the slick creeps from the blade into the wound.
The creature takes an additional 3d6 void damage.
Additionally, it becomes afflicted with festering corruption.
It cannot regain Hit Points (from any source: spells, abilities, potions, natural healing) until the start of your next turn after the spell ends.
Once the slick has triggered against a creature, it cannot trigger against any creature again.

After the slick triggers, the spell continues to suppress healing on the affected creature until the duration expires, even if the weapon is dropped.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The void damage on trigger increases by 1d6.

**Heightened (5th)** The slick can trigger twice (once per hit, up to two separate creatures) before it is expended.
