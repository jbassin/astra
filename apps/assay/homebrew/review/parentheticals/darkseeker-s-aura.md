# Darkseeker's Aura

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(Interact + Cast a Spell)`

**Rank 4** · acid, attack, concentrate, manipulate, planara

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** self  
**Targets:** you  
**Duration:** 1 minute  

---

A swirling mist of corrosive black vapor from the plane of Gehenna engulfs your body.
For the duration, any creature that hits you with a melee Strike takes 2d6 acid damage.

As a 2-action activity (Interact + Cast a Spell), you can focus the vapor into a single corrosive burst against one creature within melee reach.
Make a melee spell attack roll against that creature.
On a hit, the target takes 8d6 acid damage.
This attack ends the aura immediately; the vapor is entirely consumed.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The aura's retaliation damage increases by 1d6, and the focused attack damage increases by 2d6.
