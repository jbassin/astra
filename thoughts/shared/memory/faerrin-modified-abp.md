The Faerrin campaign (the live Foundry world) uses a modified Automatic Bonus Progression. Josh
does not hand out potency, striking or resilient runes, and items' numeric item bonuses are off
the table as rewards (GM Core: "Items that normally grant an item bonus to statistics or damage
dice no longer do, other than the base item bonus to AC from armor"). Property runes are in play:
the PCs' sheets carry them.

**Why:** a loot suggestion for the ESOTERICA fight offered +2 weapon potency runes; Josh corrected
it (2026-11-16).

**How to apply:** when suggesting treasure, pick items for their abilities (property runes, spells,
activations, consumables, currency), not their item bonuses. Ask what the modifications are before
relying on a stock ABP detail.
