# Forceful Onslaught

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(these are refreshed each turn; they do not stack with themselves)`

**Rank 7** · concentrate, force, kosmoturgy, manipulate, polymorph

**Traditions:** divine, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 willing creature  
**Duration:** 1 minute  

---

You infuse a creature with explosive gravitational momentum, pressing beyond the limits of ordinary physiology.
For the duration, the target gains a +2 status bonus to attack rolls, Athletics checks, and Fortitude saving throws.
The target deals an additional 2d6 force damage on all weapon Strikes.
At the start of each of the target's turns, it gains 10 temporary Hit Points (these are refreshed each turn; they do not stack with themselves).
Finally, if the target is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it gains the Dying condition as normal, but it doesn't fall Unconscious until the start of its next turn and can act normally until then.
It attempts recovery checks as normal, and it can't be stabilized by any means while it remains conscious this way.

---
**Heightened (9th)** The additional Strike damage increases to 3d6, and the first time the target is reduced to 0 Hit Points during the duration, it doesn't gain the Dying condition until it falls Unconscious at the start of its next turn. Effects that would kill it outright still do so.
