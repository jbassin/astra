# Summon Heart

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(or functional equivalent)`

**Rank 6** · concentrate, death, kosmoturgy, manipulate, void

**Traditions:** divine, occult  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Targets:** 1 living creature  
**Defense:** basic fortitude  

---

You exert a crushing gravitational pull on a living creature's vital core, compressing and tearing inward.
The target takes 11d10 void damage.
If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, it is immediately slain; its heart (or functional equivalent) is ripped free and appears in your free hand.
This is a death effect.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage.
If this reduces the creature to 0 Hit Points, it is immediately slain as described above.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10.

---

**Heightened (+1)** damage +1d10
