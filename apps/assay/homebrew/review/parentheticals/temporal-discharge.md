# Temporal Discharge

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(consumed)` `[cost.value]`
> - `(a unique word or phrase)`
> - `(until the caster's next daily preparations plus 1 day at rank 5, etc., to a maximum of 1 week at rank 7)`

**Rank 4** · chronomancy, concentrate, manipulate

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 10 minutes  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 non-creature object  
**Defense:** basic reflex  
**Duration:** until discharged or until your next daily preparations  
**Cost:** a gemstone worth at least 25 gp (consumed)  
**Rarity:** rare  

---

You spend ten minutes channeling magical energy into a touched object, storing a temporal charge within it.
When you cast the spell, choose one of the following damage types: acid, cold, electricity, fire, sonic, or void.
Also speak a command word (a unique word or phrase) in a language you know.
The charged object is otherwise unchanged in appearance; a successful Recall Knowledge or Seek check against your spell DC reveals the presence of a magical aura.

**Trigger:** The first time a creature touches the object without speaking the command word within 6 seconds of touching it, the stored energy discharges, requiring the triggering creature to attempt a basic Reflex save against your spell DC and take 10d6 damage of the chosen type.

The object is not destroyed by the discharge.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6, and the duration extends by 1 day per additional rank (until the caster's next daily preparations plus 1 day at rank 5, etc., to a maximum of 1 week at rank 7).
