# Carnage

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(roll once, apply to all)`
> - `(roll once, apply to all; these Strikes likewise count as a single attack for your multiple attack penalty)`

**Rank 8** · concentrate, force, incapacitation, kosmoturgy, manipulate, teleportation

**Traditions:** arcane, divine  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** self  
**Targets:** all creatures in your reach after teleporting  
**Defense:** basic fortitude  
**Duration:** sustained up to 1 minute  

---

You tear open the fabric of space and fling yourself across the battlefield in a single brutal arc, bringing your weapon to bear against everything in reach.
When you Cast this Spell, you immediately teleport up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space you can see, then make a single melee Strike against every creature adjacent to you in your new location (roll once, apply to all).
These Strikes count as a single attack for your multiple attack penalty, which increases only after all of them resolve.
Each creature struck also takes 6d6 force damage from the spatial rupture with a basic Fortitude save; your weapon Strike resolves normally regardless of the save's outcome.

When you Sustain this Spell, you teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see, then make a single melee Strike against every creature adjacent to you (roll once, apply to all; these Strikes likewise count as a single attack for your multiple attack penalty).
Each creature struck also takes 4d6 force damage with a basic Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature takes no force damage from the spatial rupture.

**Success** The creature takes half force damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full force damage.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double force damage and is Stunned 1.
