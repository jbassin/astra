# Monstrous Copy: Wail

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(success / failure / critical failure)`

**Rank 6** · auditory, concentrate, gestalt, incapacitation, manipulate, mental, morph, sleep, sonic

**Traditions:** primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** self  
**Area:** 20-foot emanation  
**Defense:** fortitude  

---

You draw upon your knowledge of the Banshee, reshaping your vocal cords into a monstrous approximation of its spectral wail.
You unleash a bone-chilling keening cry that pierces the mind of all who hear it.
Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude saving throw.
A creature that is Deafened is immune to this spell.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes 4d8 sonic damage.

**Failure** The creature takes 8d8 sonic damage and is Stupefied 2 for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 16d8 sonic damage, is Stupefied 4 for 1 minute, and is Unconscious for 1 minute.
The creature can attempt a DC 15 flat check at the end of each of its turns to recover from the Unconscious condition.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage on each tier increases by 1d8 / 2d8 / 4d8 (success / failure / critical failure).
