# Shape Modify: Speed

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(or another polymorph effect)`

**Rank 3** · concentrate, gestalt, manipulate, morph

**Traditions:** primal  
**Cast:** 1 action  
**Range:** self  
**Targets:** you  
**Duration:** until your wild shape ends  

---

You draw upon the collective's memory of wildshaping to refine your current form.
You must be in wild shape (or another polymorph effect) to Cast this Spell; if you are not, the spell fails with no effect.
You elongate and lighten the limbs of your current form for explosive mobility, trading defense for speed.
For the duration, your Speeds increase by 20 feet for all movement types available to your current form, but you take a -2 circumstance penalty to AC.
This spell ends immediately when you leave your polymorphed form.

---
**Heightened (5th)** The Speed increase rises to 30 feet, and the AC penalty is reduced to -1.

**Heightened (7th)** The Speed increase rises to 40 feet, and the AC penalty is removed entirely.
