# Legend Killer

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(abilities with the mythic trait)`

**Rank 5** · chronomancy, concentrate

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 1 action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** fortitude  
**Duration:** 1 minute  

---

Time thickens around a mighty creature, dragging at its limbs and dulling the edge of its greatness.
The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** For the duration, the creature can't use mythic abilities (abilities with the mythic trait) or spend Mythic Points.

**Failure** As success, and the creature also can't take reactions.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature is Stunned 1.
