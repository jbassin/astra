# Repetitious Trauma

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(or mental if no damage type is known)`

**Rank 5** · chronomancy, concentrate, manipulate, mental

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** will  
**Duration:** sustained up to 1 minute  

---

You twist the strands of time to force a creature to relive a damaging moment over and over.
On the round you cast this spell and each round you Sustain it, the target must attempt a Will saving throw.
The damage type each round is the most recent damage type dealt to the target before this spell was cast (or mental if no damage type is known).
Each round's save is rolled separately.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected this round.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes 8d6 damage of the relived type.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is Stunned 1.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.

---

**Heightened (+1)** damage +2d6
