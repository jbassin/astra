# Rewind and Playback

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(your choice of how many to restore, up to the maximum)`

**Rank 3** · chronomancy, concentrate, healing, manipulate, void

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 willing living creature other than yourself  

---

You reach through time and pull a creature's wounds backward, rewinding the damage into the past and redirecting it through yourself as a conduit.
The target regains up to 3d8+24 Hit Points (your choice of how many to restore, up to the maximum).
You then take void damage equal to the number of Hit Points restored; this damage is not subject to resistances, immunities, or damage reduction, though it can be healed normally after you take it.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The maximum healing increases by 1d8+8.
