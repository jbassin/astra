# Darkseeker's Restraint

> **Flagged parentheticals in this spell:**
> - `(against your spell DC)`
> - `(instead of @UUID[Compendium.pf2e.conditionitems.Item.Grabbed]{Grabbed})`
> - `(not restrained)`

**Rank 5** · concentrate, incapacitation, manipulate, planara

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** 90 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** will  
**Duration:** 1 minute  

---

You summon stygian binding chains from the bleak realm of Gehenna to seize a creature you can see within range.
The target must attempt a Will save.
While Restrained by the chains, the target automatically fails Reflex saves against your spells and is Off-Guard.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected and is temporarily immune to Darkseeker's Restraint for 24 hours.

**Success** The target is not restrained, but is Grabbed by the chains for 1 round.
It can attempt to Escape (against your spell DC) as part of a 2-action Escape attempt.

**Failure** The target is Grabbed and takes 3d8 bludgeoning damage.
While Grabbed by the chains, the target takes 3d8 bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns.
The target can attempt a new Will save at the end of each of its turns; on a success it is freed.

**Critical Failure** The target is Restrained (instead of Grabbed) and takes 6d8 bludgeoning damage immediately.
While Restrained, the target takes 6d8 bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns.
The target can attempt a new Will save at the end of each of its turns; on a success it is only Grabbed (not restrained).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial and per-turn damage increase by 1d8.
