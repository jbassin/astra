# Grey Frost

**Rank 7** · cold, concentrate, incapacitation, manipulate, planara

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal  
**Cast:** 2 actions  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** fortitude  
**Duration:** 1 minute  

---

You channel the brutal cold of the Grey Waste into a creature you touch, flash-freezing its body from the outside in.
The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes 5d8 cold damage.

**Failure** The creature takes 3d8 cold damage and is afflicted with Grey Frost at stage 1.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 6d8 cold damage and is afflicted with Grey Frost at stage 2.

**Grey Frost**; **Saving Throw** Fortitude against your spell DC; **Maximum Duration** 1 minute (the affliction ends if the spell ends, except at stage 3); **Stage 1** Restrained as ice encases the creature's limbs (Escape DC equals your spell DC; successfully Escaping shatters the ice and ends the affliction) and 3d8 cold damage (1 round); **Stage 2** Petrified as the creature becomes a statue of ice (1 round); **Stage 3** the creature is permanently Petrified, frozen solid.
The ice outlasts the spell and remains until the creature is gradually thawed, and it stays frozen only while the ambient temperature remains below freezing.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial damage on a failure increases by 1d8, and Stage 1's per-round cold damage increases by 1d8.
